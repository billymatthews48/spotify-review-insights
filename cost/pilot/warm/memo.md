Prioritize usability, especially disruptive ads and missing controls. It leads the actionable product areas in the saved totals: usability has 13 complaints and severity sum 37, compared with playback at 8 and 28, and billing/support at 9 and 26. Keep playback reliability under focused investigation because the #1 baseline issue, PLB-99, is a playback catch-all with 6 complaints, severity sum 21, and priority score 21 [C1][C2][C3].

## Why

Treat it as a baseline signal to diagnose, not a single feature prescription.
