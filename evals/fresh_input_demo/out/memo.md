Cancellation language is intent, not observed churn or causal evidence. Catch-alls may combine distinct issues: BIL-99 includes premium controls, while OTH-99 is nonspecific. Facts report no quarantined or pending-unclassified records, but issue-level catch-alls remain. The available issue categories limit what can be inferred.
