Topic (pick the highest-severity problem; tie = first mentioned)

access
Login, signup, password or account access
usability
Navigation, controls, layout, queue/playlist management, ad interruptions
playback
Playback failure, crashes, lag, connection failures, audio quality, resource use
downloads
Downloading, saved music, offline listening, disappearing downloads
catalog
Missing songs/artists, search/discovery, recommendations, lyrics availability
billing
Price, charges, subscriptions, paywalls, premium entitlement; explicitly premium-only controls
support
Contacting support and the support response
other
General praise/criticism, unrelated content, no supported specific topic

Positive review: first specific praised feature; general praise = other. A paid plan mentioned alone is not billing.

Intent (precedence order)

cancellation
Explicitly leaving, uninstalling, cancelling, or threatening to
complaint
Negative experience, incl. mixed praise/criticism; generic "bad app"
request
Desired change without a reported failure
praise
Positive
unclear
Bare boycott slogans, unrelated or meaningless text

Severity

1
No reported problem: praise, neutral/unclear, pure feature request
2
Dislike, generic criticism, minor annoyance, cosmetic; no functional loss
3
Degraded/restricted function; some use or workaround remains
4
Clearly blocked core task (can't log in, can't play music)
5
Explicit serious financial, privacy or data harm. Price, crash or anger alone is not enough

Stars are hidden on purpose: label the text. Cancellation intent does not raise severity by itself.
