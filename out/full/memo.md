# Next-quarter product effort: usability

Prioritize usability, beginning with excessive ad interruptions (USB-01). Usability has the largest complaint total among the actionable areas shown, and its leading ranked issue is specific enough to guide product work. Treat the overall #1 issue, OTH-99, as a broad baseline signal—not as a feature-level roadmap item.

## Why
OTH-99 is the overall #1 issue at 35769 complaints [C1], with mean severity 1.830747 [C2], but its definition is general dissatisfaction with no specific feature. The usability area totals are 13516 complaints, severity sum 37865, mean severity 2.801495. USB-01 has 7520 complaints [C3], mean severity 2.798271 [C4], and priority score 21043 [C5]. Reviews describe frequent or loud ads, including “Just got 4 minutes of ads straight” (cd8bf064-c32e-400e-906e-a4d900819bbd) and a reviewer saying they uninstalled and returned to Pandora (515e302e-bc7b-4e54-94b6-de3990912ec0). That last statement is expressed intent, not observed churn.

## Alternatives considered
Playback totals are 8199 complaints, severity sum 27479, mean severity 3.351506. It ranks below usability by area complaint total; its ranked PLB-01 issue has priority score 4880 [C6], though reviews describe songs stopping (be6e9ce7-c336-4195-ae25-6e14174bc611).

Billing/support totals are 7859 complaints, severity sum 22279, mean severity 2.834839. It also ranks below usability by area complaint total. BIL-99 has priority score 9312 [C7], but it is a catch-all; a review about lyrics being moved behind Premium illustrates the broad mix (e0fed482-caa7-4d0d-8af1-6703844881da).

## Evidence
Reviews are consistent with the classified themes, but are illustrative examples, not representative samples.

## Limitations
The classification is incomplete: facts report 100050 in-scope records, 237987 completed records, 422622 pending unclassified records, and 13 quarantined records. Reviews are self-selected and historical (2022-05-17 to 2023-11-15). OTH-99 and other -99 issues are catch-alls; OTH-99 names no specific feature, so it is a baseline rather than a direct product target. Cancellation language is expressed intent, not observed churn.
